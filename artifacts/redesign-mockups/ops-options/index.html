Why this mattered

The old complete action did three things: write a completion, move next due by the frequency, and walk rotation_order to the next member.

Without rotation, bins stay “Jordan’s Tuesday” forever. That’s how houses get quietly unfair.

Old app: overdue is a date; escalated is a threshold. Bins escalate the same day. House admin waits two days. Last done stays on the card so the house remembers who last actually did it.
