# Homepage review — 27 September 2026

Scope: the current editorial homepage at desktop and 390px mobile width. User goal: understand what Commune does, see whether it fits their group, and start a trial. These are visual and content findings from the captured page, not user research or a full accessibility test.

## Walkthrough

1. **Opening hero — strong.** The doorway photograph, direct headline and single prominent action make the first screen feel human and clear. The navigation and secondary link are small against changing photography. [Desktop](./01-hero.jpg) · [Mobile](./07-mobile-hero.jpg)
2. **Three feature cards — clear but repetitive.** The cards make the group, split and payment record tangible. They explain the same three steps shown again in the dark walkthrough, and on mobile each large card adds substantial scroll. [Desktop](./02-features.jpg) · [Mobile](./08-mobile-content.jpg)
3. **Photo stories and product walkthrough — strong separately.** The dark interactive walkthrough is the clearest proof of the product. The two photo stories add warmth, but both centre on meals; another setting would better show the range of shared life. [Stories](./03-life-photos.jpg) · [Walkthrough](./04-walkthrough.jpg)
4. **Use cases, emotional bridge and pricing — mixed.** The use-case selector demonstrates one product for different groups. The standalone blue-sky bridge looks good but feels detached from the decision to choose a plan. Pricing repeats the same feature list on each card, making differences in group and member capacity harder to scan. The plan photos could imply that Standard is only for homes and Pro is only for trips. [Use cases](./05-use-cases.jpg) · [Bridge](./06-moment.jpg) · [Pricing](./05-pricing.jpg)
5. **Journal and ending — good.** The journal adds useful depth and the closing image gives the page a warm finish. Footer links become quite small and faint over the photograph. [Journal](./09-journal.jpg) · [Ending](./10-closing.jpg)

## Priority changes

1. Keep the interactive walkthrough as the main product explanation. Shorten or repurpose the three feature cards so the page does not tell the same story twice, especially on mobile.
2. Give pricing one short “all plans include” line, then highlight only the limits and specific benefits that change by tier. Make clear that homes, work and trips are uses of Commune, not exclusive plan categories.
3. Replace one early meal photograph with people doing shared work or travelling, and attach the blue-sky emotional bridge more clearly to the use-case story or the closing.
4. Increase the size and contrast of small footer text and check navigation contrast on every rotating hero image.

## Limits

Still screenshots do not establish keyboard behaviour, screen-reader quality, motion preference handling, or actual trial completion. Those need a separate interaction check.
