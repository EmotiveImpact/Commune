Commune — Onboarding v5

10 screens. Three acts: sell (1–4), acquire (5), setup (6–9), celebrate (10). Reframed for users who've already authenticated. Subtype + a contextual sell mid-flow give the user a sense of progress AND make the product feel personalised. Closes with a real celebration moment.

v5 changes from v4

  • fixed Removed "Get started / I already have an account" from screen 1 — onboarding fires post-login, those CTAs were nonsense. Replaced with personalised "Welcome, [Name]" + "Continue".
  • fixed "Free" claim removed from sell pages. Pricing screen exists for that. Sell screens focus on what the product does, not what it costs.
  • added Subtype step (screen 7) — flows naturally from Type. Each type has 3–4 subtypes (Flatmates → Flat / House / Halls / Other; Couple → Married / Cohabiting / LDR; Travel → Holiday / Business / Roadtrip; Workspace → Vendors / Project / Agency / Other).
  • added Contextual sell (screen 8) — micro-sell tailored to the chosen type+subtype. "Here's how Commune works for flatmates in a flat: 1) Rent auto-splits, 2) Chore rota, 3) Settle monthly." Reduces drop-off because the user sees the product through their actual situation.
  • added Celebration screen (screen 10) — confetti, big "Welcome to Commune", glowing icon, "next up" preview tile pointing at the first thing to do. Real moment of delight.
  • unchanged Acquisition step (5), pager mechanics, Back/Skip semantics.

What this needs to ship

Schema: add acquisition_source TEXT NULL column to profiles via 1-line migration.

Type+subtype copy: store the contextual-sell strings in packages/core/src/onboarding-copy.ts as a record keyed on {type}-{subtype}. The screen reads from this. Default fallback if subtype is unrecognised: use the generic type-level copy.

Pager: screens 1–4 use react-native-pager-view (Reanimated-3 compatible, 16-line dep). Screens 5–9 are stacked steps. Screen 10 is its own dedicated route or final state.

Celebration mechanics: 9 confetti pieces fall from the top with staggered useSharedValue animations on mount. Big icon scales in (0.5 → 1.0 with spring). Headline + sub fade in 200ms after icon. Total animation length: 800ms. Don't loop — one-shot moment.

Skip flow: "Skip tour" on screens 1–4 jumps to step 5 (acquisition). "Skip" on 5/8 is per-step skip. Setup steps (6, 7, 9) cannot be skipped — Skip is hidden. Final screen: no skip, just "Open Commune".
