What a complete product reference must cover

Use this as the screen-and-state checklist, not a claim that every example here is shipped.

Home
One large selected commune, smaller others. Personal next actions across communes. Distinguish a partial read from an empty account.
This Hub
Photo and place identity, your money, immediate needs, dated week list, useful notes and activity. Keep section icons in empty states too.
Expenses
Two-column saved-expense boxes, filters, payer, total paid versus your share, due calendar and settled / partial / overdue states.
Settle
You owe versus you’re owed, actual-payer receipt confirmation, relevant share details, pending / failed / limited reminders and receipt history.
Notes
Board and reader. Type-specific Wi-Fi, contacts, access details and free text. Editable contact roles, long names, masked secrets and no-match filters.
Funds
Goal, recorded contributions, pledges, remaining amount. Editing, cancellations, corrections and archive / restore must explain their consequences.
Recurring
Next-due calendar, payer and split. Scheduled / due / paused / recording states. Record one occurrence, never imply automatic payment.
Operations
Week / People / Month, shared date calendar, unassigned / yours / overdue / done / recurring tasks, failed updates and no double completion.
People
Member roles, email invitations plus shareable links, invite pending / expired / accepted and clearly bounded member-management permissions.
Settings
Account versus commune settings, browser-local photo/payment hints, preferences, permissions and save feedback. Role and reminder allowance share one sidebar line.
Search
Explicit commune scope, grouped exact-record results, typing / pending / no matches / failure, keyboard navigation and focus restored on close.

Responsive and interaction contract

  • Retain the same hierarchy on a phone. Columns stack, labels wrap, actions stay reachable; do not merely shrink the desktop page.
  • The Nudge design target stays alongside the ledger on wide screens, then follows the selected share when narrow. Opening moves focus to the action; closing restores it.
  • Keyboard: Left / Right, Home / End switch these three tabs. Escape closes the Nudge preview except while sending. Every control needs visible focus.
  • Minimum 44px touch targets on mobile. Colour is never the only way to express failure, completion, selection or overdue status.
  • Disable duplicate writes while pending. If a write committed but refresh failed, refresh the saved record. Never offer a duplicate create.
  • Keep known content visible during refresh. Remove private content after confirmed loss of access. No loading-as-empty, invented success or automatic retry of an uncertain send.

Standalone visual prototype · example household · no account changes or emails. This reference illustrates the design language; its example controls do not save data. Open the real app to use the implemented workflows.
